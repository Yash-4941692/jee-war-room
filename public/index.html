JEE War Room — the JEE Main study tracker built for two

JEE War Room is a free, ad-free web app for JEE Main and JEE Advanced aspirants who study in pairs. It turns a day of preparation into numbers you can act on: hours and questions logged, chapter-wise syllabus progress, daily execution against your targets, mock-test accuracy, a mistake notebook, a study streak, and a transparent projected-AIR estimate — with a buddy beside you to keep it honest.

📝 Activity log

Log lectures, revision, DPPs, previous-year questions, homework, focus sessions and custom metrics in a few taps. Every entry rolls up into today's questions and study minutes.

⏱️ Focus timer

Start a 25/45/90-minute session, pause and resume it, and see exactly how many minutes of real study (and how much distraction) the week actually contained.

🎯 Daily targets & execution %

Plan what today looks like, mark each target done, partially done or missed, and get an execution percentage instead of a vague feeling of "I studied".

📚 Chapter-wise syllabus tracker

The full JEE Main chapter list for Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, with per-chapter status and lecture counters, so you always know what is untouched.

📈 Mock test analysis

Record total score and the subject split, and track accuracy and score trends across every test you take — part tests, coaching tests and full JEE Main mocks.

🔁 Mistake notebook

Log the questions you got wrong with an error type — concept, calculation, silly mistake, misread, guess, time pressure — and see which pattern keeps costing you marks.

🏆 Streak, XP and levels

A day counts when you actually studied. Streaks make consistency visible, and XP/levels reward the boring, repeated work that ranks are built on.

⚔️ Buddy duels

Two aspirants, one room: compare weekly minutes, questions and target execution, and message each other. Only the blocks you explicitly share are visible to your buddy.

🔒 Private by default

Passwords are stored as one-way PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashes, sessions as digests with a 30-day expiry. No ad networks, no social feed, no third-party tracking pixels.

How it works

  1. Create your account — pick a name and password and set your JEE Main exam date for the countdown.
  2. Log your day — activities, focus sessions, targets and mistakes take seconds to record.
  3. Review the week — study minutes, questions, syllabus progress, accuracy and projected AIR, in one screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is JEE War Room free?
Yes. It is free to use, has no ads and does not sell data. It is a small, independent project, not a coaching institute.
Does it predict my JEE rank?
No — and it never claims to. The projected AIR is a transparent, deterministic estimate built from your own logged consistency, targets, practice, revision and mock performance, shown with the word estimate on screen. It is a direction-of-travel signal, not a rank prediction.
Which syllabus does the tracker cover?
The JEE Main chapter list for Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics ships with every account (61 chapters by default) and you can add, hide or rename chapters to match your own coaching material.
Do I need a study partner to use it?
No. Everything works solo, and you can connect a buddy later with a friend code. Buddy views respect your per-block sharing switches — study time, targets, questions, streak, XP, score, projected AIR and distractions are each opt-in.
Where is my data stored?
Server-side in a libSQL (Turso) database — not in your browser, so it survives clearing site data and syncs across your devices. Passwords are one-way hashes and session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 digests.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. The interface is built mobile-first with a bottom navigation bar, and it can be added to your home screen like an app.

Not affiliated with the National Testing Agency (NTA), JEE, or any coaching institute. Study hard — the tracker just keeps score.
